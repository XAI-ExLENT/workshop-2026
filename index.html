Workshop Overview

The workshop will feature a panel discussion on the growing importance of AI/ML and experiential learning, along with keynote presentations from experts applying AI/ML across diverse application domains. XAI participants will showcase their projects through poster presentations, creating opportunities to share their work, receive feedback, and connect with professionals in the field. A networking luncheon will provide additional opportunities for interaction among participants, speakers, MSU students, faculty, and industry representatives while encouraging conversations about higher education, research, internships, and career pathways.

The workshop will include discussions and activities focused on the following:

  • Emerging applications and future directions of AI/ML
  • The role of experiential learning in AI/ML education and workforce preparation
  • Academic and industry perspectives on essential AI/ML skills
  • Responsible, ethical, secure, and trustworthy use of AI/ML
  • Student research and experiential learning projects
  • Higher-education, internship, research, and career pathways in AI/ML
  • Networking and collaboration among students, educators, researchers, and industry professionals

This workshop is organized as part of the XAI: Experiential Learning in AI/ML for Future Innovators project, supported by the U.S. National Science Foundation ExLENT award #252591.

About XAI

XAI: Experiential Learning in AI/ML for Future Innovators is an NSF-supported workforce-development project that equips students with practical AI/ML skills through structured coursework, hands-on projects, industry internships, and professional mentorship. The program connects participants with academic and industry experts while preparing them for advanced education and careers in AI/ML.
