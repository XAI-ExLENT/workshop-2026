Welcome

Advancing explainable AI in research and practice.

Welcome to the XAI Workshop 2026 at Mississippi State University. This workshop brings together students, researchers, faculty, and industry professionals to explore Explainable Artificial Intelligence and Machine Learning applications across different fields.

The workshop will highlight practical AI/ML use cases, emerging research directions, and applications of artificial intelligence in STEM.
