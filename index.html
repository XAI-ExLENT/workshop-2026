Welcome

Welcome to the XAI Workshop 2026 at Mississippi State University. This workshop brings together students, researchers, faculty, and industry professionals to explore Explainable Artificial Intelligence and Machine Learning applications across different fields.

The workshop will highlight practical AI/ML use cases, emerging research directions, and applications of artificial intelligence in STEM.

AI/ML Use Cases in Different Applications

Healthcare

AI and machine learning applications for healthcare, medical imaging, and decision support.

Cybersecurity

Explainable AI approaches for threat detection, intrusion detection, and security analytics.

IoT & Edge AI

Intelligent AI systems for connected devices, edge computing, and real-time applications.

Computer Vision

Machine learning and deep learning applications for image and video understanding.

AI/ML Applications in STEM

Explore how artificial intelligence and machine learning are transforming science, technology, engineering, and mathematics.
